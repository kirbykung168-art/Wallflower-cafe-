A small manifesto

For the quiet ones — the wallflowers.

Good things come to those who search. We opened in June 2016 as Oneday Wallflowers, a flower shop tucked into the old Pure Luck kombucha brewery on Soi Nana — Casa Lapin's "second location in the Old Town." We grew upward from there: flowers on the walls, cake in the case, coffee from a single-origin filter. The whole building is the cafe now. Bring a book. Bring a friend. Bring no one at all. When the cafe lamps come on at half-past five, the spiral staircase keeps climbing — past flower-tube chandeliers and fairy lights, past raw brick and timber, up to the rooftop garden. Garden-of-Eden cocktails, soft amber lighting, the slow turn of a vinyl record on a Friday night. Order the ROSY STRAIGHT BLUSH. Ask for a railing seat.

It used to be a florist; they brought the flower-arranging idea into a cafe that's perfumed with the scent of flowers. Chinatown welcomes the rustic rooftop bar it never knew it needed.
— Tazzjang Aey, Wongnai (10 Oct 2024, translated from Thai) — BK Magazine, 12 Nov 2018
